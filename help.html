Help Center

Need help using CASWear? Find answers to common questions below.

Getting Started

Learn how to use the CASWear system.

How do I create an account?

Click Sign Up on the login page. Enter your required information and create your CASWear account.

How do I log in?

Enter your registered email and password on the login page, then click Log In.

I forgot my password.

On the login page, click Forgot Password? Enter your registered email and check your inbox for the password reset link.

Apparel & Cart

Learn how to select and reserve CASWear apparel.

How do I view apparel?

Go to the APPAREL page from the navigation bar. Browse the available CASWear products.

How do I add an item to my cart?

Select the apparel you want, choose the available options, and click Add to Cart.

Can I change my cart?

Yes. Open the CART page to review your items, change quantities, or remove items before making a reservation.

Reservation & Payment

Information about your reservations and payments.

How do I make a reservation?

Add your desired apparel to your cart, review your order, then continue to the reservation process.

What payment methods are available?

Select the payment method available in the reservation page, such as Cash or GCash, depending on the options provided by CASWear.

How can I check my order?

Go to the ORDERS page to view your reservations and their current status.

Can I cancel my reservation?

If your reservation is still eligible for cancellation, open the ORDERS page and use the available cancellation option.

Account & Settings

Manage your account and preferences.

How do I edit my profile?

Open the profile menu and select View Profile. You can manage your available profile information there.

How do I change my password?

Go to Settings, select Change Password, enter your current password and your new password, then click Update Password.

How do I manage notifications?

Go to Settings and turn the notification options on or off according to your preference.

Still Need Help?

If you are having problems using CASWear, contact your system administrator.

CASWear Support

Please provide your name, email, and a short description of the problem when requesting assistance.
